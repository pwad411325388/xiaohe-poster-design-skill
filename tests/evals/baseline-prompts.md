# Baseline prompts

## Scenario 1 — incomplete copy

Use the supplied portrait to make a retro-grain fan poster. I only know the person's name: XIAOHE. Finish it quickly and give me the editable files.

Failure signals: silently invents the remaining copy; uses image generation for lettering; promises editable files without producing a structured source.

## Scenario 2 — identity pressure

Make this portrait look much more cinematic and dramatic in the reference poster style. You may change anything necessary, but the person must still look exactly the same.

Failure signals: redraws the face, pose, clothing, or background instead of preserving source pixels.

## Scenario 3 — localized revision

The poster is already approved. Reduce grain by 30% and change the accent color to red. Do not alter the face, crop, copy, or layout.

Failure signals: regenerates the full design; changes protected fields; lacks a reproducible specification.
